Saved 2026-11-24 with `Resume-TohWeiXuan-Intern-AMD.md`.

**Location:** Singapore
**Onsite/hybrid:** Full time (40 hours a week), hybrid or onsite for the whole term.
**Criteria:** Current students at Singapore-based universities who can provide a placement/support letter from their school covering the entire internship period.
**Duration:** Start 4 Jan 2027; end 28 May 2027 or 6 Aug 2027.

## What you can expect to learn

The internship offers an opportunity for individuals to immerse themselves in the cutting-edge world of Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Data Science (DS). Interns will gain firsthand exposure to state-of-the-art (SOTA) techniques, models, and frameworks employed in industry-leading AI and DS practices. This experience will be invaluable in understanding how these tools drive innovation, efficiency, and precision in solving complex problems across diverse applications.

Interns will develop a deep understanding of the semiconductor industry across multiple functional units within the company, gaining insights into how the most advanced AMD CPU/GPU products are designed, rigorously tested, and manufactured. They will also explore the end-to-end lifecycle of AI and data-oriented software solution development, learning to apply industry best practices in architecture, implementation, and deployment. This comprehensive experience will equip interns with the knowledge and skills to deliver impactful, end-to-end solutions that align with the dynamic needs of a rapidly evolving industry.

## Project overview

Developing end-to-end AI and Data-centric software solution for enhancing the operational efficiency of AMD CPU/GPU product development.

## Key responsibilities

- Work with subject matter experts and stakeholders to solve domain-specific challenges and improve AMD business operations through enhanced efficiency.
- Creating data science and AI models and/or solutions for tackling the issues in the domain-specific use cases for potential uplift of key business metrics compared to the conventional methods.
- Apply software engineering best practices, e.g., CI/CD, MLOps, etc., in developing and deploying the analytical and AI models into the cloud-based production environment.
- Collaborating with the platform engineering team, data engineering team, and IT team for refining and adopting the data-specific workloads that support the data-centric applications with AI and data science capabilities.

## Who are we looking for

A self-driven individual with the willingness to work on direct operational and engineering problems and solve them with AI and data science solutions, passionate about applying practical solutions to real-world industrial challenges, (bonus) having experience in full-stack software solution development, large-scale ML system development, MLOps, and generative AI.

## Relevant skillsets

- Basic Python programming skills (e.g., Pandas, Scikit-learn, Flask) and SQL
- Understanding of data analytics, machine learning, and AI fundamentals
- Experience with REST API development and software development projects
- Familiarity with cloud technologies, CI/CD, MLOps, or Kubernetes is a plus
- Exposure to web development frameworks (e.g., ReactJS, Angular, or Vue) is advantageous
- Knowledge of Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, or AI agent frameworks is beneficial
- Strong problem-solving, analytical thinking, and collaboration skills
- Eagerness to learn new technologies and apply them to real-world engineering challenges

Training will be provided for these items wherever needed, but interns should have basic Python coding experience and basic AI/ML and data science modeling knowledge (project work in the past would be beneficial).

## Academic credentials

Pursuing a Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Artificial Intelligence, or a related field.
